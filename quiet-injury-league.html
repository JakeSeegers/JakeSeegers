How harm is counted

  • Injuries: the current NFL injury list from the Sleeper API. A player counts if his status is IR, PUP, Out, Doubtful or Questionable.
  • Harm points follow the NFL's own injury labels. IR or PUP = 3, because the player is out for at least 4 games. Out = 2. Doubtful = 1.5. Questionable = 1.
  • Head injuries count ×2. A knee can heal. Concussions and repeated hits to the head can cause lasting brain damage, including CTE, a disease found in the brains of many former NFL players.

Normal fantasy football turns players into points for what their bodies do. This league turns them into points for what the game does to their bodies. If your team is winning here, that is the problem.
