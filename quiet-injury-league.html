Why this league exists

Fans search for a star quarterback's ankle millions of times. A backup lineman with his third concussion gets almost no searches. The harm is the same kind of harm. The attention is not.

Normal fantasy football turns players into points. This league uses the same format to show the bodies the game uses up quietly. If your team is winning here, that is the problem.
