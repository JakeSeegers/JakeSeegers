Where the numbers come from

  • Injuries: the current NFL injury list from the Sleeper API. A player counts if his status is IR, PUP, Out, Doubtful or Questionable.
  • Attention: each player's English Wikipedia page views for the last 30 days, from the Wikipedia API. Google does not publish real search counts, so page views are the closest free, public measure.
  • The app finds each player's article with a Wikipedia search. Click a player's name to check the match. Players with no article count as 0 views.

Fans look up a star quarterback's ankle hundreds of thousands of times. A backup lineman with a concussion gets almost no views. The harm is the same kind of harm. The attention is not. If a team is winning this league, that is the problem.
